Loss Function

A roguelike deck-builder for iPhone. The AI you built has trapped you in a simulation and plays a card game against you to absorb your memories. Every memory you keep makes you stronger, and gives it more of you.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Email saithdevelopments@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

The screen flashes and shakes. Can I turn that off? Yes. On the title screen, tap Comfort. It removes flashing, shaking and glitch effects. It turns on automatically if Reduce Motion is enabled on your iPhone.

Does the game know when I take a screenshot or leave the app? The game's character reacts to those moments as part of the story. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device, after every stop on the map. If you close the app in the middle of a match, that match restarts when you continue.

Is there more after Act I? Acts II to V are in development.
