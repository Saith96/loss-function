Loss Function: Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026.

Loss Function is a single-player game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

What the app does

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app makes no network requests.
  • Saved on your device only. Your progress and settings are stored locally on your iPhone and are removed if you delete the app.
  • In-game reactions. The game's character reacts when you take a screenshot, leave the app, or your battery is low. It only reads that these events happened, on your device, at that moment. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Purchases. If a purchase is offered in a future version, it is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Children. The game does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
